Top 20 Breeds

  • French Bulldog – Small, affectionate, and adaptable, ideal for city living with low to moderate exercise needs.

  • Retriever – Friendly, energetic, and intelligent, great for families and service work.

  • Golden Retriever – Loyal, gentle, and highly trainable, often used as therapy or guide dogs.

  • German Shepherd – Intelligent, protective, and versatile, excellent for work and family protection.

  • Dachshund – Small, lively, and courageous, known for their long body and playful personality.

  • Poodle – Highly intelligent and hypoallergenic, available in standard, miniature, and toy sizes.

  • Beagle – Friendly, curious, and energetic, great for families and active households.

  • Bulldog – Calm, affectionate, and low-energy, suited for apartment living.

  • Boxer – Playful, loyal, and protective, excellent with children.

  • Rottweiler – Strong, confident, and loyal, requires proper training and socialization.

  • Pembroke Welsh Corgi – Intelligent, affectionate, and active, known for herding instincts.

  • Australian Shepherd – Energetic, intelligent, and trainable, ideal for active owners.

  • Miniature Schnauzer – Friendly, alert, and low-shedding, good for families and apartment.

  • Yorkshire Terrier – Small, lively, and affectionate, suitable for city living.

  • Doberman Pinscher – Intelligent, loyal, and protective, excellent for security and family.

  • Cane Corso – Powerful, loyal, and protective, requires experienced handling.

  • Shih Tzu – Small, affectionate, and low-energy, ideal for indoor living.

  • Great Dane – Gentle giant, friendly and protective, requires space and care.

  • Cavalier King Charles Spaniel – Affectionate, gentle, and adaptable, great lapdog.

  • German Shorthaired Pointer – Energetic, intelligent, and versatile, excellent for active families.
